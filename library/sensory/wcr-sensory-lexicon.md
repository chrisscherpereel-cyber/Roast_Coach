# WCR Sensory Lexicon 2.0 — extraction notes

**Source file caveat.** The PDF supplied as "2.0, 2025" is dated 2017 throughout: the cover reads "VERSION 2.0 — 2017" and the copyright page reads "Second Edition, 2017". Cite it as 2017.

## Structure

109 attributes in 17 categories: Taste Basics, Fruity, Sour/Acid, Alcohol/Fermented, Green/Vegetative, Stale/Papery, Earthy, Chemical, Roasted, Cereal, Spices, Nutty, Cocoa, Sweet, Floral, Amplitude, Mouthfeel. Each entry is a name, a one-sentence definition, and one to four physical reference standards; each reference carries a preparation instruction and, usually, an intensity score labelled *Aroma* or *Flavor*. 246 references in total. Sour, Bitter, Salty and Sweet are printed twice — in full under Taste Basics, then as cross-references. Meaty/Brothy is the only attribute with no reference standard in 2.0.

## The intensity scale

A 0–15 point scale. Anchors: 0 none, 2 barely detectable, 4 identifiable but not very intense, 6 slightly intense, 8 moderately intense, 10 intense, 12 very intense, 15 extremely intense. Values are absolute — Hazelnut 3.5 means the same in Texas and Bangalore. The 24 FlavorActiV standards have no score: they are "validated and approved for use as a quality representation of the named flavor attribute, but [have] not undergone assessment to determine a Sensory Lexicon intensity score."

## Intended use

WCR is explicit that it "is not a replacement for cupping or other sensory tools": it is purely descriptive, with no good/bad ranking. A trained panel of 5–7 tasters (six to nine months' calibration) holds two or three orientation sessions, prepares the references, brews to a fixed protocol, then smells aroma references and tastes flavor references before scoring the sample against them. Roughly 15 minutes per sample on 35–40 attributes; four to six samples per session; each scored three times blind.

## 2016 → 2.0

The attribute list, categories and definitions are unchanged. The stated change is "the addition of new, globally available references for 24 attributes" — shelf-stable FlavorActiV flavour standards for Sour, Bitter, Salty, Apple, Grape, Coconut, Pineapple, Acetic/Butyric/Isovaleric acid, Fermented, Peapod, Fresh, Papery, Musty/Earthy, Musty/Dusty, Moldy/Damp, Phenolic, Petroleum, Brown Spice, Almond, Vanillin, Floral, Jasmine. Also observed: Meaty/Brothy lost its Campbell's Beef Broth reference (Flavor 9.5); Vegetative's asparagus reference was de-branded; "Absolute Vodka"→"Absolut"; "Raisins"→"Raisin"; "Spice brown"→"Brown spice"; the 2016 ISBN was dropped.
